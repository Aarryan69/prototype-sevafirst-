THE CHALLENGE

India's Produce Loss is a Decision-Making Problem

Quality Uncertainty

Time-Sensitive Losses

Delayed handling reduces recovery opportunities. Perishable batches require faster decisions.

🔗

Fragmented Recovery

Lower-grade batches may lack viable buyers. Small quantities face unfavorable transport economics.

Rule-Based Engine

Decision Rules (Transparent)

📅
Harvest Age

Batches ≥4 days old get +30 urgency points. Tomatoes deteriorate faster in ambient conditions.

🔍
Visual Grade

Grade C/D batches get +40 urgency points. Grade B gets +20. Grade A is baseline.

🌡️
Storage Condition

Ambient storage adds +15 urgency points vs cool/cold chain storage.

📦
Batch Size

Small batches (<20 kg) get a flag for coordinated pickup to improve economics.

🛡️
Safety Flag

Grade D batches are automatically routed to composting/biogas — never food channels.
